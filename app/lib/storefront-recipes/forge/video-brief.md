# Forge motion briefs

## Video brief: hero / exploded tool motion

- Duration: 6 seconds, seamless loop, 16:9.
- Scene: an unbranded hand tool separates into a small set of mechanically coherent components over a steel drafting table, pauses as orange numbered callouts align, then reassembles along the same axes.
- Camera and light: locked overhead camera, hard workshop light, restrained shadows, parchment drawing lines.
- Exclude: trademarks, readable specifications, people, invented performance claims, impossible parts, sparks, text, logos, and UI.

## Video brief: hero-alt / jobsite context

- Duration: 6 seconds, seamless loop, 16:9.
- Scene: gloved hands place an unbranded tool beside a printed construction drawing on a tidy active work surface; a square and pencil shift naturally as the tool settles.
- Camera and light: shoulder-height documentary view, practical daylight, steel and orange accents, no dramatic camera move.
- Exclude: trademarks, unsafe tool use, readable project details, faces, invented claims, text, logos, and UI.

## Video brief: pdp-detail / material and mechanism macro

- Duration: 5 seconds, seamless loop, 4:3 safe center crop.
- Scene: macro study of an unbranded metal adjustment mechanism moving through one physically plausible cycle while surface texture and tolerances remain consistent.
- Camera and light: slow lateral macro move, controlled workshop light, dark steel background, one orange reference mark.
- Exclude: trademarks, measurements, certifications, impossible articulation, floating parts, text, logos, and UI.

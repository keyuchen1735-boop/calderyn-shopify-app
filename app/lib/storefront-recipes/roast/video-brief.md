# Roast video brief

All three roles are eight-second, silent, locked-camera 16:9 loops. Keep the same espresso-brown, cream, and safety-orange art direction. No labels, logos, people, hands, text, claims, or product packaging.

## `hero` — roasting

A tight steel cooling tray receives freshly roasted coffee beans in one continuous, physically coherent stream. Beans keep stable shape and scale, settle under gravity, and never melt, multiply, or reverse. Warm roaster light; quiet cream negative space at left. The last frame must match the first.

## `brew-context` — brewing context

A glass server beneath a pour-over cone on a cream brew bench. A narrow water stream remains continuous, steam rises naturally, and the coffee level changes only imperceptibly across the loop. Locked exposure and framing; no jumping liquid, disappearing equipment, or reversed steam.

## `bean-grind` — bean and grind macro

Split macro study of whole beans and freshly ground coffee on a marked steel surface. Only a subtle raking orange light moves. Particle size, bean count, shadows, and boundaries stay stable; no crawling grounds, morphing beans, or new objects.

## Approval gate

Approve only exact masters that pass technical inspection and full-loop visual review. Create poster, WebM, MP4, manifest, and proof records only after approval. Until then all nine logical keys remain unresolved.

# Haven video briefs

All films are 8 seconds, 16:9, seamless, silent, and contain no people, text, logos, labels, measurements, delivery claims, or invented product details. Furniture geometry, upholstery seams, and joinery remain stable throughout.

## hero — Slow interior dolly

A slow dolly through a quiet ash, walnut, and clay-toned room containing one unbranded lounge chair and low table. Natural window light moves gently across fixed furniture; the camera remains level and architectural.

## hero-alt — Lived-room context

A restrained lateral move across an unbranded dining table, two chairs, a folded linen, and one ceramic vessel in a warm lived room. No hands or people enter; all furniture proportions and placements remain fixed.

## pdp-detail — Upholstery and joinery macro

A macro focus pull across blank woven upholstery into a walnut joint on one unbranded furniture object. Fibers and wood grain remain physically coherent; no hardware, seams, or edges morph.

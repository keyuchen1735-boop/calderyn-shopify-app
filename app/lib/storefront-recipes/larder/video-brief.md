# Larder video direction

All three eight-second loops use the same warm paper, tomato, olive, glass, and wood palette. They are decorative recipe media only; live merchant product imagery stays bound through `product.primaryImage`.

## hero / ingredient cascade

A locked, tactile cascade of tomatoes, olives, lemons, garlic, beans, and rosemary into unlabelled glass jars. The composition reserves warm paper space for the Larder headline.

## hero-alt / kitchen context

A slow lateral move through a working Mediterranean pantry: olive shelves, clear staple jars, tomato linen, and afternoon light. The scene communicates replenishment without inventing merchant products.

## pdp-detail / package texture

A macro glide across cream paper, tomato twine, unmarked olive wax, glass, linen, and wood. It supports material context beside the live product gallery, never in place of catalog images.

## Approval

The full loops were reviewed at 1280×720 for continuity, tactile motion, and identity safety. No logos, labels, letters, or readable packaging text are visible. Reduced-motion storefronts retain the approved poster frame.

# Fizz video briefs

All films are 8 seconds, 16:9, seamless, silent, and contain no people, words, logos, labels, prices, offers, or product claims. Can geometry must remain cylindrical and stable from first frame to last.

## hero — Floating can stack

Three unbranded slim sparkling-beverage cans float in a playful stack over a cobalt, coral, and citrus studio set. Crisp commercial lighting, restrained camera orbit, a few bubbles, and natural condensation. No collisions or morphing.

## hero-alt — Social table

Several unbranded slim cans sit on a cobalt outdoor table with coral napkins and citrus slices. A light breeze moves only the napkins; cans remain geometrically stable. Slow lateral camera move, no hands or people.

## pdp-detail — Condensation macro

Macro view of cold condensation and rising carbonation bubbles on one blank cobalt can beside a coral-and-citrus gradient. Slow focus pull, physically coherent droplets, fixed can silhouette, and no readable marks.

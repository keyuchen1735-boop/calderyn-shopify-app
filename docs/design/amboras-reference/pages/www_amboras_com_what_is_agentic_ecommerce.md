What's next
## An autonomous CRO engine,
running on your store.
Once a store has traction, the same agent that built it starts looking for what to improve, on its own. It finds the conversion leaks, writes the hypothesis, runs the test, ships the winner. You watch the numbers move.
01Detection
### A continuous engine watching every store.
Funnels, anomalies, friction scoring, segment discovery, correlation, cross-store percentiles. All deterministic, all running 24 / 7. Causal-inference and significance guards built in so we only act on real lifts, not noise.
ClickHouse · detectors · rollups
02Synthesis
### Signals become hypotheses you can ship.
The engine produces a ranked queue of structured signals. The LLM sits at the very end as a synthesizer, pulling qualitative context, session replays, reviews, and support tickets, and writing a human-readable hypothesis with proposed test, expected lift, and confidence. It never reads raw event tables.
Structured signals · RAG · hypothesis writer
03Cross-store learning
### Every test makes the next one smarter.
Peer benchmarks and percentile rankings let your store compare itself to thousands of others on Amboras. A growing library of past test outcomes feeds the synthesizer, so winning patterns on one store accelerate the next. Your store gets the benefit of every test the platform has ever run.
Peer benchmarks · test library · fine-tune
04Ship the winners
### A / B / n today. Bandits tomorrow.
Tests start on the surfaces that move revenue the most: PDP, homepage hero, design and copy first, then offers and pricing. They run as proper A / B / n with statistical significance gates, winners get promoted automatically, and you decide where the autonomy threshold sits.
PDP · hero · A/B/n → bandits

## Questions, answered.
How is "agentic" different from a chatbot?
A chatbot answers questions. An agent uses tools to take action. On Amboras, every chat message can become a real change to your store: a product created, a price updated, a page edited. The AI decides which tools to call, runs them, and shows you the result.
Can the AI break my store?
Risky actions need confirmation. Build steps run in a verified sandbox before anything goes live. The AI screenshots its own work and shows you the diff. You can roll back any change.
Do I have to use the AI for everything?
No. Every screen still has a normal interface. The AI is an option, not a wall. Most merchants use chat for the heavy work and the dashboard for quick edits.
Why 74 tools? Will more be added?
Each tool is a verified action with strict input validation. We add new tools weekly as we extend coverage into shipping, tax, returns, customer segments, and analytics.
What about my existing store on another platform?
Migrations are free on every paid plan. We move products, customers, orders, and SEO redirects so you keep your traffic.

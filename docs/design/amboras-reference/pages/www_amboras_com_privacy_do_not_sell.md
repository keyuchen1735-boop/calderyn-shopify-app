# Your Privacy Choices
Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information
California residents have the right under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of their personal information and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. Residents of certain other U.S. states have similar rights under their state privacy laws.
## Our practices
**Amboras does not sell your personal information.** We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months.
We do not run advertising pixels, conversion trackers, or third-party advertising analytics on amboras.com. See Section 9 of our [Privacy Policy](https://www.amboras.com/privacy) for the full list of tools we use and the categories of data each one receives.
## Submit a request
Even though we do not sell or share your information, you may still submit a formal opt-out request, a request to limit our use of sensitive personal information, or any other privacy-rights request (access, deletion, correction, portability) by emailing us at privacy@amboras.com with the subject line “Your Privacy Choices Request.”
Please include in your message:
  * The right you wish to exercise (opt out of sale or sharing, limit use of sensitive personal information, access, deletion, correction, or portability).
  * The email address associated with your Amboras account.
  * The state in which you reside.
  * Whether you are submitting the request on behalf of someone else as their authorized agent. If so, attach written authorization.


We will verify your identity before processing the request and will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (generally 45 days, with one possible 45-day extension).
## Global Privacy Control
We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as a valid opt-out of sale or sharing of personal information under California law. If you visit amboras.com from a browser that transmits a GPC signal, we treat that as an opt-out for that browser without requiring further action.
## Storefronts hosted on Amboras
If you are a visitor to a store operated by a merchant on the Amboras platform (rather than amboras.com itself), this page does not govern that storefront. The merchant is the controller of the data collected through their store and is responsible for honoring privacy-rights requests, including any opt-outs of sale or sharing. Please refer to that merchant's privacy policy for the contact information and procedure.
## Non-discrimination
You will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising any of your privacy rights. We will not deny services, charge different prices, or provide a different level or quality of service because you submitted a request.

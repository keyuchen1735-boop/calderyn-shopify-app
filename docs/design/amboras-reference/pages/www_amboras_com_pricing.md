## Questions,
answered.
Still on the fence? Talk to a human — no pitch deck, no pressure.
[Get started](https://www.amboras.com/register)
What's the difference between Launch and Grow?
Launch is one store, one operator, real Stripe checkout, your own domain. Grow adds up to 5 stores under one subscription, team seats, autonomous A/B/n testing, multi-currency, post-purchase upsells, and priority support. Most solo founders start on Launch and move to Grow once they hire or hit their first scale wall.
Can I upgrade or downgrade later?
Any direction, any time. Your data moves with you. No migrations, no data loss, prorated billing.
How does the launch guarantee work?
Your store goes live within 24 hours, or we build it with you. If you do not make a sale in your first 30 days on Launch, Grow, or Advanced, we refund every dollar.
Do you handle migration from Shopify?
Yes. Free on every paid plan. We move products, customers, orders, and redirects so your SEO stays intact. Most migrations finish inside 48 hours.
When should I move to Advanced or Enterprise?
Advanced if you run a portfolio of stores, want a dedicated CSM, white-label storefront, and custom AI fine-tuning on your brand voice. Enterprise if you need unlimited stores, SOC 2, SCIM, B2B with net terms, or a white-label platform for an agency — book a call and we tailor the plan.

## Most shop platforms hand you an empty store and say “good luck.”
The eCommerce app market has 21,000+ third-party apps because the underlying platforms do very little themselves. Reviews, loyalty, pre-orders, returns, exit popups, page builders, email — all paid add-ons. A serious merchant easily pays over $500/month in third-party app fees, and their store gets slow because of all the apps loading on every page.
Then they still have to write every product description, shoot every photo, configure DNS, set up SEO, and answer every support ticket themselves. The platform gives them a form. The work is theirs.
And when they want to ship a new offer for Black Friday, Cyber Monday, or any flash sale — they have to redesign the page, create new discounts, build new banners, sometimes spin up new products. Two weeks of work for one weekend of selling.
We rebuilt the whole stack with AI as the foundation, not a plugin. The merchant talks. Amboras does.

## Questions, answered.
Is Amboras a website builder or a full shop platform?
A full shop platform. You get products, variants, inventory, card-payment checkout, orders, customers, returns, promotions, analytics, emails, shipping, and a customer-facing storefront. The website is one piece of it.
How is this different from a legacy platform with an AI app bolted on?
On legacy platforms, AI is a plugin you bolt on top of an interface designed for clicking through forms. On Amboras, AI is the interface — it’s what an [AI-native shop system](https://www.amboras.com/what-is-agentic-ecommerce) actually means. There is no separate “AI app” because every page already has it. Adding a tool means giving the AI a new capability — not asking the merchant to install an app.
Do I have to use AI for everything?
No. Every AI-generated thing — products, descriptions, images, brand colors, promotions — is editable. The AI builds a strong default in two minutes; you tweak whatever you want.
Where does my data live?
On Amboras infrastructure. Card payments process directly into your bank account. Your store data is exportable at any time — no lock-in beyond what every shop platform requires.
What about migrating from another platform?
We move products, customers, orders, and redirects so your SEO stays intact. Most migrations finish inside 48 hours. Free on every paid plan.

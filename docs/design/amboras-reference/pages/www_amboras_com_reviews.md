## Reviews that _read themselves._
### AI summary at the top
Three bullets distilled from every approved review. Updated the moment a new one lands. Shoppers see the gist before scrolling.
### Fake-review filter
Pattern recognition catches review-bombing, paid-for praise, and bots. Flagged reviews never publish; the merchant is notified in the admin queue.
### Auto-Q&A from reviews
When a question gets asked, the answer is mined from the past review corpus and quoted with attribution. No merchant inbox to manage.

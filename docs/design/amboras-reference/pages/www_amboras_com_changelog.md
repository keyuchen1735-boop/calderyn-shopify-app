Changelog
# What’s new in Amboras.
Small improvements to the admin, shipped continuously. Here’s what landed in the last few weeks.
May 25 – June 1
  * Jun 1
### Razorpay, natively.
Connect Razorpay from Settings → Integrations and accept UPI, cards, and netbanking from Indian shoppers at checkout.
  * Jun 1
### Affiliate marketing with GoAffPro.
Set up a referral program in minutes — connect GoAffPro and start tracking partner sales without leaving the admin.
  * May 31
### Free shipping, per region.
Set different free-shipping thresholds for each country you sell in — $50 over US orders, ₹2000 over Indian orders, your call.
  * May 31
### Customer tab polish.
A cleaner customers table with filters that stick and faster loads when you have thousands of records.
  * May 30
### The AI knows which page you’re on.
Ask the chat assistant for help and it already sees what you’re looking at — no need to re-explain whether you mean Orders, Products, or Analytics.
  * May 28
### Invite your team.
Add teammates to your store with Admin or Member roles and per-store permissions. Email invites, token-based onboarding, more controls coming next.
  * May 28
### Apple Pay, in two clicks.
Connect your custom domain to Apple Pay from the admin — no terminal commands, no signed files.
  * May 28
### Payment methods, redesigned.
A cleaner Stripe Connect onboarding flow that mirrors the rest of Settings.
  * May 26
### Skydropx in Mexico.
Connect Skydropx from Settings → Integrations and ship across Mexico with rated labels and tracking.
  * May 26
### No more trial.
New stores skip the trial and pick a plan up front. Cleaner signup, no countdown timer. Existing trials keep running.
  * May 25
### Talk to a human.
A new call widget on amboras.com — book a 20-minute walkthrough with a founder directly from the marketing site.


May 19 – 24
  * May 24
### A checkout that converts 20% higher.
Rebuilt end-to-end around trust. Real payment-method logos (Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Amex) replace generic icons, country and dialing code auto-detect on the phone step, security and shipping reassurance live above the fold, and error states stop dead-ending the buyer. Measured against the old flow on real traffic, the new checkout lifts paid conversion by roughly 20%.
  * May 24
### Custom product metadata.
Attach any key/value metadata to a product and use it to power custom storefront blocks, integrations, or merchandising rules. No schema forks, no plugins required.
  * May 24
### Backorders, finally.
Sell past zero. Flip "allow backorder" on a variant and Amboras keeps taking orders while inventory catches up, with the right messaging on the PDP and in the cart.
  * May 24
### Add a region in two clicks.
New add-region dialog with a curated country catalog. Country and currency editing now properly lock once a store has multiple regions, so a live setup can never be broken by a stray click.
  * May 23
### Multi-region management.
Add or remove regions and countries straight from the admin. Payment providers mirror automatically onto every new region, so a fresh market is ready to take orders the moment you create it.
  * May 23
### Unguessable store URLs.
New stores ship with a crypto-random suffix in their slug, so preview URLs can’t be discovered by walking sequential names. Quiet but important security upgrade.
  * May 22
### Queue messages while the AI works.
Stop waiting on the agent. Stack up follow-up prompts and they run in order, animate in as they start, and auto-pause the moment the AI asks you a question.
  * May 21
### BigCommerce and WooCommerce CSV imports.
Two more migration paths into Amboras. Drop in a BigCommerce or WooCommerce product export and we map, validate, and import the lot, same pipeline as the Shopify importer.
  * May 21
### Variant-level discounts.
Promotions and buy-get rules can now target specific variants, not just whole products. Run a Black Friday discount on a single color or size without touching the rest of the catalog.
  * May 21
### Guided shipping setup.
A new "show it how" tour walks you through zones, rates, and delivery options in a few clicks. The fastest path from new account to a store that can actually ship.
  * May 21
### Attach images straight from the welcome screen.
Drop reference images into the very first message of any chat. The agent uses them to seed your brand, products, or design direction before it builds anything.
  * May 20
### Paid-ads onboarding funnel.
A quiz-style onboarding flow tuned for cold ad traffic, with UTM-based routing so signups always land in the right experience. Built to convert paid clicks, not just curious browsers.
  * May 19
### Plan drawer opens on mobile.
Upgrading from a phone now opens the plan picker as a clean drawer instead of bouncing through extra screens. Pick, pay, done.
  * May 19
### Free-tier guardrails on the Designer.
Hitting the Store Designer on the free plan now surfaces the upgrade prompt right where the action is, instead of failing silently after you’ve already filled in the form.


May 12 – 18
  * May 18
### Discover section in the admin.
A new home for plugins and features with live previews and one-click install dialogs. Browse what Amboras can do without leaving your dashboard.
  * May 18
### Mockup generator.
Describe a scene and the AI delivers a polished product mockup, ready to drop into PDPs, ads, or social. No design tools, no stock photos.
  * May 18
### Book a Call from the admin header.
A Book a Call button now sits in the admin header, so you can grab a slot with the founders without leaving your dashboard. No support queue, no email back-and-forth.
  * May 18
### Theme gallery + template carousel.
Each theme template now ships with three preview screenshots, and the create-theme dialog uses a paginated, animated carousel so you can compare looks side-by-side before committing.
  * May 18
### Drag-drop product media.
Reorder a product’s images by dragging them. The first image becomes the PDP hero. No more juggling upload order.
  * May 18
### Automatic currency conversion.
Prices convert to the visitor’s region on the fly, and min/max promotion rules now respect the resolved currency. One catalog, every market.
  * May 17
### TikTok Ads, natively.
Connect TikTok Ads from Settings and ship server-side conversion events the same way we already do for Meta. Cleaner attribution, lower CPA on TikTok spend.
  * May 17
### Start a theme from a template.
Pick a curated template instead of starting from scratch. The registry of starter themes lives inside the admin and grows every week.
  * May 17
### Color-coded to-dos.
The dashboard punch-list now shows status at a glance: done, in progress, waiting on you. Less scanning, more shipping.
  * May 17
### 30-day free trial.
Start a 30-day trial with a card on file, or skip the trial and go straight to a paid plan. Pricing card and trial-aware CTAs got a fresh polish at the same time.
  * May 16
### Mobile chat, redesigned.
The AI chat on phones is now a draggable sheet with snap points at half-height and full-height. Opens from any to-do tile, closes with a tap, never gets in the way.
  * May 16
### Live activity, everywhere.
Every AI tool that touches your store now lights up an activity dot in the relevant admin tab, color-coded by status. You always know what just changed, and where to go look.
  * May 15
### AI image enhancement, before and after.
Upgrade a product photo and see the result side-by-side on your dashboard. Powered by the new gpt-image-2 model. Most photos go from "snapshot" to "ad-ready" in one click.
  * May 15
### Pause, resume, or cancel any AI build.
Agent runs now survive deploys, restarts, and SIGTERM with full state intact. Bail out mid-run and pick up later from exactly where you stopped. No more "build vanished" mysteries.
  * May 15
### A founder texts you after signup.
New merchants get a short SMS drip from the founders within minutes of finishing onboarding. Real humans, real numbers, gated to production with dedup so nobody ever gets a duplicate message.
  * May 14
### A/B testing for storefronts.
First pass of native experiments. Run two variants of any storefront against each other and let the data pick the winner.
  * May 14
### Plugins across themes.
Manage which plugins are installed per theme. Draft and live themes can carry different plugin sets without stepping on each other, so you can prototype risky changes safely.
  * May 14
### AI lints its own code.
The agent now runs a fast static check between edits, catching syntax and type errors before they reach the build. Fewer broken previews, faster iterations.
  * May 13
### Activity dots in the admin.
Products tab shows a dot when AI edits are in flight, Store tab surfaces a deployment banner. You always know what’s happening behind the curtain.
